🎓 How The Payout Validation Gate Protects Capital

Compliance Rail
Before a withdrawal is approved, ApexBet re-executes a real-time POST /api/identity/identify check against the registered identity. If the recipient's bank account or age fails to verify, the withdrawal is automatically frozen before funds leave the company vault.
💡 Key takeaway for your boss: This eliminates internal fraud, account takeovers, and unauthorized payouts to unverified third-party bank accounts.
